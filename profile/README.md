# SimorqCare

SimorqCare builds practical software for mental health care teams.

Our mission is to help clinics, therapists, receptionists, supervisors, and patients work with less friction, fewer lost details, and more trust in the daily care process.

## What We Build

- Simorq: clinic operations for psychology and therapy practices.
- Nafas: a reflection and self-awareness companion.
- Daroonegar: medication tracking and care continuity tools.

## Goals

- Make clinic front desk work faster, calmer, and more reliable.
- Keep appointments, patients, payments, forms, messages, and history in one clear workspace.
- Help therapists focus on care instead of scattered admin work.
- Give clinic owners simple visibility into revenue, workload, no-shows, and follow-up.
- Protect sensitive care data with clear access, history, and accountability.
- Build Persian-first products that match real local clinic workflows.

## Principles

- Practical over bloated.
- Trust over magic.
- Human review over blind automation.
- Clear workflows over scattered tools.
- Care teams first.
